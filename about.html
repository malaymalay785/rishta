About Rishta PK

Rishta PK is a free matrimony website for Pakistani singles and families in Pakistan and abroad. You create a profile, discover suitable people, send a friend request and chat privately once the request is accepted. There are no fees and no hidden charges.

Open Rishta PK

How Rishta PK works

  1. Sign in with Google. No password to remember.
  2. Create your profile. Four short steps: basic details, religion and career, family, and your partner wishes, plus a real profile photo.
  3. Browse profiles. Use the All, Girls and Boys switch to find people you like.
  4. Send a friend request. The request stays open for 24 hours.
  5. Chat. When the request is accepted, the conversation opens in the Chat tab.

What a profile contains

Age, height, marital status, city and country, mother tongue, sect, caste, education, occupation, family details, a short introduction and what you want in a partner. Anything left empty is shown as "Not added" so everybody knows what was not shared.

Safety and respect

You can block or report any profile at any time. A blocked person cannot see you or message you. Chat is possible only after both sides agree. Please never send money or private documents to someone you have just met online, and involve your family in important decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rishta PK really free?

Yes. Creating a profile, sending requests and chatting are free.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 18 or above who is looking for a marriage partner.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Settings, then Delete account. Your profile is hidden at once and all your data is permanently deleted after 15 days.

How do I contact support?

Open Settings, then Support center, or write to lartkapagal@gmail.com.
