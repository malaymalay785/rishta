How to Find the Right Marriage Partner Online

Finding a life partner is one of the biggest decisions in anyone's life. An online website can help you meet new people, but it cannot choose for you. This guide gives simple and practical steps to look for a suitable partner with patience, respect and the support of your family.

Start with clear thoughts about yourself

Before you look at other profiles, spend a little time thinking about your own life. Ask yourself what really matters to you. Is it faith, family time, career, living in a certain city, or a calm and simple home? When you know your own needs, it becomes easier to see who may suit you. Write down three or four things that are very important, and a few things that are nice to have but not necessary. This list is only for you, and it will keep you from being carried away by a nice photo or a few kind words.

How to look through profiles

On Rishta PK, the Home tab shows profile cards of members who have completed their profile. You can use the All, Girls and Boys switch at the top to change what you see. Take your time to scroll. Tap a card to open the full profile instead of judging only by the photo.

A full profile shows basic details, a short "About me", religion and career, family details and partner wishes. Read every part slowly. A green dot shows that the person is online now, and a red dot shows when they were last seen. This only tells you about activity. It does not tell you anything about character.

How to compare important information

It is natural to compare profiles, but compare the things that truly matter for a married life. Here are some useful points to look at:

  • Age and marital status: check that these fit what you and your family are comfortable with.
  • City and country: think about where each of you lives now and where you would live after marriage.
  • Education and work: look for a similar level of understanding and a stable way of earning.
  • Religion and lifestyle: sect, prayer and habits like smoking should be clear for both people.
  • Family details: joint or nuclear family, house and family status help you picture daily life.
  • Partner wishes: read what the person hopes for. See if it is close to what you can offer, and be honest with yourself.

Do not treat a profile like a shopping list. A person is more than a list of boxes, and a profile only shows what someone chose to share.

Why compatibility matters

Compatibility means two people can live together with understanding. It does not mean being the same in everything. Strong marriages often have two people who respect each other's differences and agree on the big things. These usually include values, how to treat family, how to handle money, and what to do in hard times.

Beauty, income and status can change over the years. Kindness, honesty, patience and respect usually stay with a person. When you read a profile and later talk to someone, try to notice these qualities too.

Sending a request with care

When a profile feels suitable, tap Add to send a friend request. The request stays open for 24 hours. It is better to send a request only to people you really find suitable, not to everyone you see. When someone sends you a request, open their profile, read it fully and think before you accept. You can decline with no explanation if it does not feel right. Only after a request is accepted can the two of you chat.

What questions to ask

Once the chat opens, start politely and slowly. Begin with simple questions such as how the person's day was, what they do, and what they enjoy. After a few conversations, you can move to more meaningful topics. Here are some good questions:

  • What does a normal day look like for you?
  • What are you hoping for in married life?
  • How close are you with your family, and how do you see their role after marriage?
  • Where would you like to live in the future?
  • What are your goals for the next five years?
  • How do you usually solve a disagreement?

Listen carefully to the answers. Ask in a calm way and do not make the other person feel questioned like in an exam. For a longer list of topics, read our guide on things to discuss before marriage.

Why you should not rush

A good rishta needs time. If someone pushes you to decide in a few days, to move to another app, or to keep the conversation secret from your family, slow down. People who are serious do not mind waiting for you and your family to feel sure.

Take time to meet, to ask relatives and to think. It is also fine to say "I need more time" or "I do not feel this is the right match". Saying no politely is better than a marriage that causes years of sadness. If one request does not work out, that is not a failure. It is part of finding the right person.

How families can be involved

In Pakistan, marriage joins families as well as two people. It helps to involve your parents or elders early. You can show them the profile, share what you have talked about, and listen to their thoughts. They may notice things that you miss. When the chat goes well, the next step is usually an introduction between the families, followed by meetings in a safe place with family present.

Families should also respect the choice of the young person who will live the marriage. The best decisions usually come from open and kind talk between everyone. For more help, read our Pakistani family rishta guide.

Red flags to consider

These signs do not always mean a person is bad, but they are reasons to be careful and ask more questions:

  • The person asks for money, gifts or bank details.
  • The story in the profile and the story in the chat do not match.
  • The person avoids sharing any real details, or refuses every call or meeting without a clear reason.
  • The person talks about love or marriage very fast and pressures you.
  • The person is rude, angry, or speaks badly about family and former partners.
  • The person asks you to hide the relationship from your family.
  • The person sends links or asks for private photos and documents.

If you see these signs, you can use Report, Block or Unfriend inside Rishta PK. Our safety and security page explains how each one works.

A simple path to follow

  1. Build an honest profile. See our guide on how to create a good rishta profile.
  2. Read profiles slowly and send requests to people who truly seem suitable.
  3. Chat politely and ask meaningful questions over time.
  4. Share the progress with a trusted family member.
  5. Meet through the families, in a safe place.
  6. Take the final decision calmly, with prayer and advice, and never under pressure.
