Rishta PK – Frequently Asked Questions

Here are short, honest answers to the questions people ask most. If you cannot find your answer, open Settings in the app and use the Support center.

About Rishta PK

What is Rishta PK?

Rishta PK is a free matchmaking website for Pakistani singles and families. You can create a profile, browse other profiles, send friend requests and chat once a request is accepted.

Is Rishta PK free?

Yes. Creating a profile, browsing, sending requests and chatting are free on Rishta PK.

Is Rishta PK only for people in Pakistan?

Rishta PK is made for Pakistani singles and families. When you create your profile you can choose Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, USA, Canada or Other as your country.

Can Pakistani families living abroad use Rishta PK?

Yes. Pakistani families and singles living in other countries can sign in with Google and create a profile in the same way.

Profile and matches

How do I create a profile?

Sign in with your Google account. A short form with 4 steps opens: basic details, religion and career, family, and about you with partner wishes. A profile photo is required. You must be 18 or older. Our guide on how to create a good rishta profile can help you write it well.

How can I find a marriage partner on Rishta PK?

Open the Home tab, use the All, Girls or Boys switch, and tap a card to read the full profile. When you like someone, send a friend request. Take your time and involve your family before making any serious decision. Read more in how to find the right marriage partner.

Can I edit my profile?

Yes. You can edit your profile up to 5 times in 30 days, and you can change your profile photo once every 3 days. Check your details carefully before saving.

Requests and chat

How do I send a request?

Tap the Add button on a profile card. The button changes to Sent. The request stays open for 24 hours and then expires if the person does not answer.

When can I chat with another user?

You can chat only after the other person accepts your friend request. After that, the chat opens in the Chat tab for both of you.

Safety and account

How can I report a suspicious profile?

Tap the Report button on the profile card and write a short reason. Our team reviews reports. You can also use Block to stop seeing that person.

How can I block someone?

Tap Block on the profile card. A blocked person cannot see you and you cannot see them. You can unblock them any time in Settings under Blocked users.

How can I stay safe online?

Never send money to anyone you meet online, do not share private documents early, and tell a trusted family member about your conversations. Read our full safety and security page and online rishta safety tips.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and choose Delete account. You must confirm twice and write a reason. Your profile is hidden right away and your data is permanently deleted after 15 days. If you log in within 15 days you can cancel the deletion.
