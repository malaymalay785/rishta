How Rishta PK Works

Rishta PK is a free website where Pakistani singles and families can create a profile, meet suitable people and talk with respect. This page explains every step in simple words, from your first sign in to your first chat.

Step 1: Sign in with Google

You do not need to make a new password. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Google account. Rishta PK uses this only to know who you are and to keep your profile safe. You can log out at any time from Settings.

Step 2: Create your profile

After you sign in for the first time, you will see a short form with 4 steps. Each step has a few simple questions.

  • Basic details: male or female, name, age, height, marital status, children, city, country, mother tongue and complexion.
  • Religion and career: sect, caste or family name, prayer, hijab or beard, smoking, education, job and monthly income.
  • Family: family type (joint or nuclear), family status, house, father's and mother's work, and brothers and sisters.
  • About you and your partner wishes: a few lines about yourself, the age range and other things you hope for in a partner, and your profile photo.

A profile photo is required. You can pick one from your gallery or take a new one with your camera. Only a few key fields are compulsory. If you leave other fields empty, they show as "Not added" on your profile.

Check your details carefully before you save. You can edit your profile 5 times in 30 days, and you can change your photo once every 3 days.

Step 3: Browse profiles on Home

The Home tab shows profile cards of people who have finished their profile. Each card shows a photo, a name and whether the person is online. A green dot means online now. A red dot means offline, with the time they were last seen.

At the top you can switch between All, Girls and Boys. Tap a card to open the full profile and read everything the person shared. A blue tick next to a name means Rishta PK has marked that profile as verified.

The buttons on each card

  • Add: sends a friend request.
  • Message: opens the chat, but only after the person accepts your request.
  • Report: lets you tell our team about a profile that looks fake, rude or unsafe.
  • Block: hides that person from you, and hides you from them.

Step 4: Send a friend request

When you like a profile, tap Add. The button changes to Sent. The request stays open for 24 hours. If the other person does not answer in that time, it expires and goes away. You can send a new request later.

Step 5: What happens after a request

People who receive a request see it in the Friends tab. They can open your profile first, then choose:

  • Accept: you both become friends and a private chat opens in the Chat tab.
  • Decline: the request is removed.

Take your time when you receive a request. Read the profile slowly and decide what feels right for you and your family.

Step 6: Chat

The Chat tab lists everyone who accepted a request. Tap a name to open the conversation. Messages arrive live while the chat is open. You can see small ticks next to your messages: one tick means sent, two ticks mean delivered, and blue ticks mean seen. If you allow notifications, you will also get an alert for new messages and requests.

If you no longer wish to talk, you can use Unfriend inside the chat. This removes the chat for both people. You can add each other again later if you both want to.

Daily streak

Rishta PK has a small daily streak to help active members. If you send friend requests to 5 different people in one day, your streak grows by one. Streaks of 7, 30 and 100 days give Bronze, Silver and Gold badges. This is only a fun reward. Please send requests only to people you truly find suitable.

Why honest information matters

Marriage is a serious promise between two people and two families. If a profile has wrong age, job, marital status or family details, it can hurt people and waste everyone's time. Write true information, use your own recent photo, and share only what you are comfortable making public. Honest profiles build trust, and trust is the base of a good rishta.

Your account and privacy

In Settings you can read the how-to guide, see and unblock people you blocked, contact support, open our social media pages and log out. You can also delete your account. Your profile is hidden right away, and all your data is permanently deleted after 15 days. If you change your mind, log in within 15 days and cancel the deletion. For more details, read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
