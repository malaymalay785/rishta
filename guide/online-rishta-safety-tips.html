Online Rishta Safety Tips

Most people who look for a rishta online are sincere. But a few people use matchmaking websites to cheat or hurt others. These tips are simple habits that help you stay careful without becoming afraid. Use them on Rishta PK and on any other website or app.

No tip can remove all risk. Use your own judgment, talk to your family, and stop any conversation that makes you uncomfortable.

Fake profiles

A fake profile is made by a person who hides their real identity. They may use someone else's photo, a false job, or a made-up story. Here are signs that a profile may not be real:

  • The photo looks like a model or an actor, or looks edited a lot.
  • The person has only one photo and refuses to share any other.
  • The details in the profile change when you chat. For example, the city or job is different.
  • The person never agrees to a video call or to meet with family, and always has a new excuse.
  • The profile looks too perfect: very rich, very beautiful and very eager.

One sign alone does not prove anything. If you see two or three together, slow down and be careful. You can also ask politely for a short video call. A real person with honest intentions usually does not mind.

Financial scams

Money is the most common tool in online fraud. The scammer first makes you feel close, then asks for help. Common stories include:

  • "I am stuck at the airport and need money for a fee."
  • "My parent is in hospital and I need urgent help."
  • "I want to send you a gift or money, but you must pay a small charge first."
  • "Let us invest together in a business or a plot. I know a very good chance."
  • A person abroad who says a parcel is stuck at customs.

A person who truly wants marriage does not need your money. Do not send cash, mobile load, bank transfers or gift cards to someone you have not met through your families.

Requests for money

If someone asks you for money, even a small amount, take these steps:

  1. Do not send anything and do not argue for long.
  2. Tap Report on the profile and write what happened.
  3. Tap Block so the person cannot contact you again.
  4. Tell a trusted family member.
  5. If you already lost money, contact your bank or mobile wallet company at once, and report to the police or cybercrime authorities in your country.

Emotional manipulation

Some people do not ask for money at first. They try to control your feelings. They may say "I love you" within days, make you feel special, and then make you feel guilty if you slow down. They may say things like "If you really cared, you would send me your photo" or "Do not tell your family, they will not understand."

Real affection grows slowly and is happy to include families. Be careful when someone makes you feel afraid, guilty or in a hurry. It is always your right to say "I need time" and to end the chat.

Suspicious links and files

  • Do not click links from someone you just met online, even if the message looks friendly.
  • Do not install an app or open a file that a stranger sends you.
  • Do not type your Google password, OTP codes or bank details on any page that a chat partner shares.
  • Be careful when someone says "Please fill this form" and asks for your CNIC or family details.

Rishta PK will never ask you for your Google password in a chat. If a message claims to be from us and asks for secret codes, do not trust it.

Sharing private information

Share slowly. In the first conversations, avoid your phone number, home address, exact workplace, daily routine, CNIC, bank details and private photos. Once photos or documents are sent, you cannot take them back. Share more only when you feel safe and your families are in contact. Identity verification

You do not have to believe everything on a profile. Check it in kind and normal ways:

  • Ask for a short video call and see if the face matches the photos.
  • Ask simple questions about the person's city, work or study and see if the answers stay the same over time.
  • Ask families to meet or speak, and ask relatives if anyone knows the family.
  • Look at whether the story fits: age, job, education and lifestyle.

A blue tick on Rishta PK means the profile was marked as verified by us. It is helpful, but it is not a promise about a person's whole character, so keep using your own care.

Safe communication

It is a good idea to keep early conversations inside the Rishta PK chat. Chat is open only after a friend request is accepted, and you can end it any time with Unfriend or Block. Move to calls or other apps slowly. If someone pushes you to leave the chat on the first day, take that as a warning. Keep your messages polite, and do not reply to rude or threatening messages. Save screenshots if you may need to report them.

Safe first meetings

  • Let the families meet first, or have a family member come with you.
  • Choose a public place or a home with family present.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going, with whom and at what time.
  • Use your own transport, keep your phone charged and share your location with a family member.
  • Do not feel forced to agree to anything, and leave if you feel uneasy.

Reporting suspicious behaviour

On Rishta PK, tap Report on a profile card and write a short, clear reason. Our team reviews reports. You can also use Block to stop seeing that person, and Unfriend inside a chat.
