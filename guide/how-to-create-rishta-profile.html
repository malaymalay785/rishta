How to Create a Good Rishta Profile

Your profile is the first thing a possible match and their family will see. It is like a short, polite introduction. A clear and honest profile helps the right people understand you, and it saves time for everyone. This guide explains what to write and what to avoid.

Why your profile matters

On Rishta PK, people decide whether to send or accept a friend request after reading your profile. They do not know your voice, your family or your habits yet. Your profile has to speak for you. A profile with true, complete and respectful details builds trust. A profile with missing or confusing details makes people doubt, even if you are a very good person.

What information to include

The profile form on Rishta PK has 4 steps. Here is how to think about each one.

Step 1: Basic details

You will choose male or female and write your name, age, height, marital status, children, city, country, mother tongue and complexion. You must be 18 or older. Write your real age and your real marital status. If you are divorced or widowed, or have children, say so clearly. Many people respect honesty here, and hiding it only causes problems later.

Step 2: Religion and career

This step asks about your sect, caste or family name, prayer, hijab or beard, smoking, education, job and monthly income. Choose the answer that is true today, not the one you hope to become. For example, if you pray sometimes, choose "Sometimes". A partner who knows the real you is more likely to be happy with you.

Step 3: Family

Here you share your family type (joint or nuclear), family status, house, your father's and mother's work, and your brothers and sisters. Families care about this step a lot in Pakistan. Keep it simple and respectful. You do not need to make your family sound richer or bigger than it is.

Step 4: About you and partner wishes

This is where you write a few lines about yourself, the age range and other things you wish for in a partner, and add your profile photo. A photo is required to save your profile.

Fields that you leave empty will show as "Not added". It is better to fill in what you are comfortable with, because empty profiles get fewer replies.

Why honest information matters

A rishta is a promise between two families. If your age, job, income or marital status is not true, the truth will come out sooner or later, usually after both families have spent time and hope. That can hurt feelings and your name. Honest information also protects you, because it attracts people who really suit your life. If something feels difficult to write, it is better to discuss it openly with your family first and then write it in a calm and simple way.

How to write a good introduction

The "About me" box is your chance to sound like a real person. Three or four short sentences are enough. Write them in easy English, in the first person, and keep a warm and calm tone.

  1. Start with who you are: your work or study, and where you live.
  2. Add one or two things you enjoy, such as reading, cooking, travelling, cricket or time with family.
  3. Say what is important to you in life, such as faith, family, kindness or growth.
  4. End with a short, polite line about what kind of life you hope to build.

Example of a clear introduction: "I work as an accountant and live with my parents. I enjoy cooking, reading and weekend visits to relatives. Family and honesty are very important to me. I am looking for a kind and respectful partner to build a peaceful home."

This is only an example of the style. Please write your own words, because your own words show your real personality.

How to mention education, work and interests

  • Education: choose the highest level you have finished, not the one you are still studying for. If you are still studying, you can mention it in "About me".
  • Work: write your real job in plain words, such as "teacher", "software developer" or "shop owner". Avoid big titles that do not describe the work.
  • Income: share an honest monthly figure, or an honest range, if you choose to add it. Do not exaggerate.
  • Interests: mention only things you really do. Two or three real interests are better than a long list.

How to present marriage preferences with respect

Everyone has wishes for a partner, and it is fine to write them. The way you write them matters. Compare these two styles:

  • Harsh: "No one with a low income or small family should contact me."
  • Respectful: "I prefer a partner with a stable job and a family that values respect and understanding."

Keep your list short and focus on values and life goals rather than on appearance or status only. Use the age range, city, sect and education fields honestly, and use the other wishes box for a few calm sentences. Mention whether you are ready to move to another city or country, because this is one of the most common questions in a rishta.

Choosing your profile photo

Use a clear, recent photo of your face in good light. You can choose one from your gallery or take a new one with your camera. Avoid group photos, sunglasses, heavy filters and pictures of someone else. A natural photo helps people trust you. Remember that you can change your photo only once every 3 days, so choose it with care.

Common profile mistakes

  • Writing a wrong age, marital status or job to get more requests.
  • Using an old photo, a filtered photo or a photo of another person.
  • Leaving "About me" empty or writing only one word.
  • Writing a very long list of demands that nobody can meet.
  • Using rude, proud or negative words about other groups of people.
  • Copying a text from somewhere else instead of writing about yourself.
  • Rushing through the form and making spelling or number mistakes. Your profile can be edited only 5 times in 30 days, so check it before you save.

Privacy considerations

Your profile can be seen by other members, so think about what you share. Do not write your phone number, your home address, your CNIC or details about your daily routine. Do not mention the exact name of your workplace or school if you want to keep it private. You can share more later, when you feel safe and your family is involved. Read our safety and security page and the online rishta safety tips for more advice. If you want to leave Rishta PK, you can delete your account in Settings.

A quick checklist before you save

  • Is every detail true and up to date?
  • Is my photo clear, recent and mine?
  • Does "About me" sound like me and stay polite?
  • Are my partner wishes short and respectful?
  • Did I avoid private details such as phone number and address?
  • Did I show the profile to a trusted family member?
