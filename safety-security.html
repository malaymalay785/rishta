Rishta PK Safety & Security

Meeting people online always involves some risk. Rishta PK gives you tools to report and block, but it does not currently verify the identity of members, so your own caution is the most important protection. No website can promise that every profile is genuine.

Protect your personal information

Share only what you need. Keep your home address, CNIC, passport, bank details and workplace address private until you know the person and your family is involved. Your profile does not need a phone number, so do not add one in the About text.

Never send money

Do not send money, gift cards or mobile top-ups to someone you have not met in person, whatever the reason: an emergency, visa fees, a business problem or a gift. A genuine match does not ask a stranger for money.

Check before trusting

  • Does the profile match what the person says in chat?
  • Are the answers consistent over time?
  • Is the photo believable, and will the person agree to a video call?
  • Can the person’s family talk to your family?

Be careful with moving to other apps

Be cautious if someone quickly asks for your number or pushes you to another platform, or sends links to click. Do not open unknown links or install apps on request.

Report and block

Use Report on a card for profiles that look fake, rude or unsafe; reports go to the Rishta PK team. Use Block to hide a person from you and you from them. You can unblock later in Settings → Blocked users.

Meeting in real life

Tell a trusted family member first, meet in a public place during the day, bring a relative or friend where appropriate, and arrange your own transport. Do not accept any meeting that is secret or rushed.

Involve your family

Involving parents or elders early is a good check. See online rishta safety tips and the family guide.
