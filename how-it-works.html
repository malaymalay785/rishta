How Rishta PK Works

Rishta PK is a free matchmaking website for Pakistani singles and families. This page explains each step as it works on the site today, so you know what to expect before you start.

1. Sign in and create your profile

You sign in with your Google account, so there is no separate password to remember. After the first sign-in you fill a four-step profile: basic details, religion and career, family, and your about section with partner preferences. A profile photo and an age of 18 or above are required.

Honest answers matter. Other members read your profile before deciding to contact you, and fields you leave empty are shown as “Not added”. For tips, read how to create a good rishta profile.

2. Discover profiles

The Home tab shows member profiles. Use the All / Girls / Boys switch at the top to narrow the list. Tap any card to open the full profile. A green dot means the person is online now; a red dot shows how long ago they were last online. People you have blocked, and people you are already connected with, do not appear on Home.

3. Send and answer requests

Tap Add on a profile you like to send a request. A request stays open for 24 hours. Requests sent to you appear in the Friends tab, where you can open the sender’s profile before choosing Accept or Decline. A declined or expired request simply disappears.

4. Chat after acceptance

Chatting is only possible after a request is accepted. The accepted person then appears in your Chat tab. Each message shows a single tick when sent, double ticks when delivered and blue ticks once seen. If a conversation is not going anywhere, Unfriend removes the chat for both people and both profiles return to Home, so you can reconnect later if you wish.

5. Report, block and edit

Every card has Report and Block buttons. You can edit your profile up to five times a month and change your photo once every three days. Read Safety & Security before sharing personal details with anyone.

Optional: daily streak

If you send requests to five different people in a day, your streak grows. It is only a small habit tracker; send requests to people you are genuinely interested in.
