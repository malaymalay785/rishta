Pakistani Family Rishta Guide

Marriage in Pakistan joins families as well as two people. This guide offers practical points for parents and relatives.

Be involved, not controlling

Offer advice and do background checks, but let the bride and groom speak for themselves and have a real say.

Look at compatibility

Consider values, education, outlook and how the two people communicate, not only family name or income.

Communicate respectfully

Be polite and honest with the other family, even when you decline. A clear and kind answer is better than silence.

Education and career

Discuss openly whether the bride or groom plans to continue studying or working, and support those plans.

Talk about money honestly

Discuss expenses and expectations early and plainly. Avoid pressure around demands; keep conversations reasonable.

Avoid unnecessary pressure

Do not hurry or compare the young people with others. Let them meet in a proper setting, and listen to their concerns.

Take enough time

Verify details, meet more than once and think before accepting. See things to discuss before marriage and safety advice.
