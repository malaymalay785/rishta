Things to Discuss Before Marriage

Honest conversations before a nikah prevent many problems later. Cover these topics calmly, with family involved where appropriate.

Family expectations

How close will you be with each other’s families? How often will you visit, and who helps in difficult times?

Education and career

Will both of you work? Is further study planned? How will you support each other’s plans?

Where you will live

City, house, joint or separate family living, and whether either of you may move abroad.

Money

How you will manage income, savings, spending and support to parents. Talking about this early avoids misunderstanding.

Children and responsibilities

Whether and when to have children, and how household and childcare duties will be shared.

Lifestyle, faith and culture

Daily routine, prayer, food, social life and traditions that matter to each of you. Respect differences and be clear about what is non-negotiable.

Long-term goals

Where you both want to be in ten years. Find out whether your goals fit together. For more ideas read how to find the right partner.
