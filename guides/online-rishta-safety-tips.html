Online Rishta Safety Tips

Most people on matchmaking sites are sincere, but a few are not. These habits keep you safe while you look for a rishta online.

Fake profiles

Be careful if photos look like a model’s, the story keeps changing, or the person will not do a video call. Search your own judgement before trust.

Financial scams

Never pay for visas, tickets, customs, medical bills or gifts for someone you have not met. Scammers often invent an urgent problem.

Emotional manipulation

Be wary of someone who declares strong feelings very quickly, isolates you from family or makes you feel guilty. Healthy interest grows slowly.

Suspicious links and requests

Do not click unknown links, install apps or share one-time codes. No genuine suitor needs your passwords or bank PIN.

Sharing information

Keep your CNIC, address and bank details private. Share your number only after you feel comfortable and your family knows.

Safe first meetings

Meet in a public place with a relative nearby, tell someone where you are going and leave if you feel uncomfortable.
