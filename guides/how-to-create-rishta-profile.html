How to Create a Good Rishta Profile

A profile is the first impression a family gets of you. A clear, honest and respectful profile attracts people who are actually suitable.

Include the basics accurately

Age, height, city, education, work, marital status and family background should be correct. Small exaggerations usually come out in the first family meeting and cost trust.

Write a short, real introduction

Three or four sentences are enough: who you are, what you do, what you enjoy and what kind of life you want. Avoid copying generic lines; specific details such as your field of study or a hobby make you memorable.

Mention education, work and interests

Say what you studied and what you do now, even if you are still studying or looking for work. Interests such as reading, cricket, cooking or travel help someone picture a normal day with you.

State your preferences respectfully

Use the partner section to give an age range, city, marital status and any other wishes. Be clear but reasonable; a long list of strict conditions reduces genuine responses.

Common mistakes

  • Using an old or edited photo
  • Leaving important sections empty
  • Writing only what you want, not who you are
  • Adding phone numbers or addresses in public text

Privacy

Keep your phone number, address and ID details out of your profile. Share them only once you trust the person and your family knows. See safety tips.
