Rishta PK FAQ

Short answers to the questions people ask most. If something is not covered here, use Settings → Support center inside the app.

What is Rishta PK?

Rishta PK is a free matchmaking website for Pakistani singles and families. Adults create a profile, send requests and chat once a request is accepted.

Is Rishta PK free?

Yes. Joining and using Rishta PK is currently free and there are no paid features at the moment.

How do I create a profile?

Sign in with Google and complete the four-step profile: basic details, religion and career, family, and about you with partner preferences. A profile photo is required and you must be 18 or older.

How can I find a marriage partner?

Browse the Home tab, use the All / Girls / Boys switch, open profiles that interest you and send a request to people whose details and preferences fit yours. See our guide on finding the right partner.

How do I send a request?

Tap Add on a profile. The request stays open for 24 hours. The other person can accept or decline from their Friends tab.

When can I chat with another user?

Only after your request is accepted. The person then appears in your Chat tab.

Can I edit my profile?

Yes, up to five times in 30 days. Your profile photo can be changed once every three days.

How can I report a suspicious profile?

Tap Report on the profile card and describe the problem. Tap Block to hide the person from you and you from them.

How can I stay safe online?

Never send money, keep private documents to yourself, involve family, and meet only in public places. Read our safety page for details.

Is Rishta PK only for Pakistan?

It is built for the Pakistani community. The profile lets you choose Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, USA, Canada or Other as your country.

Can Pakistani families living abroad use it?

Yes. Families and individuals abroad can create profiles and send requests in the same way.

Does Rishta PK verify members?

No. Rishta PK does not currently verify the identity of members, so please check information yourself and follow the safety guidance.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile is hidden immediately and your data is permanently deleted after 15 days. Signing in within 15 days lets you cancel.
