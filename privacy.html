Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026. This policy explains what Rishta PK ("we", "our") collects, why, and what control you have.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and Google profile name, only to create and secure your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile details you enter: age, gender, height, marital status, city, country, language, religion, education, occupation, income, family details, partner wishes, introduction and profile photo.
  • Activity: friend requests, blocks, reports, support messages and chat messages, and basic usage such as visits and last active time.

How we use it

To show your profile to other members, let members send requests and chat, keep the site safe (reports, blocking, removing abuse), answer support messages and understand how many people use the site. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertisements based on it.

Who can see your data

Your profile details and photo are visible to other signed-in members. Your email address is not shown on your profile. Chat messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation and to our administrator when needed to handle a report or abuse.

Services we use

Google Sign-In (login), Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudinary (profile photo storage) and Cloudflare (website hosting). These providers process data only to run the service.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile in the Profile tab.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Delete account. Your profile is hidden immediately and all your data is permanently deleted after 15 days. You can cancel by logging in within 15 days.
  • Block or report anyone at any time.

Children

Rishta PK is only for people aged 18 and above. We remove accounts of anyone found to be younger.
